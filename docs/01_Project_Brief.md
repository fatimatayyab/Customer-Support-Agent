## Mission

Build an AI Customer Support Platform  that enables businesses to deploy an intelligent support agent capable of answering customer questions, performing business actions, collaborating with human agents, and continuously improving from company knowledge.

---

## Vision

Create an AI Support Agent that acts as a reliable teammate for customer support rather than a traditional chatbot.

The platform should become the first line of support for businesses while seamlessly working alongside human agents.

---

## Problem

Businesses struggle with:
- Repetitive customer questions
- High support costs
- Slow response times
- Knowledge scattered across multiple sources
- Poor customer experience
- Support teams overloaded with simple tasks

Existing chatbot solutions often:
- Give robotic responses
- Hallucinate answers
- Cannot perform business actions
- Have poor human handoff
- Provide little business insight

---

## Solution

A SaaS platform where businesses can:

- Add an AI support agent to their website
- Upload company knowledge
- Connect business tools
- Allow AI to answer questions
- Allow AI to perform approved actions
- Seamlessly transfer conversations to human agents
- Gain insights from customer conversations

---

## Core Product Pillars

1. AI Conversations
2. Knowledge & RAG
3. Business Integrations
4. Human Collaboration
5. Business Intelligence

---

## Success Criteria

The platform should:

- Answer accurately using company knowledge.
- Perform business actions securely.
- Escalate to humans when appropriate.
- Reduce support workload.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Be simple enough to deploy in minutes.
